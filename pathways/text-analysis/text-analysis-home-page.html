Text Analysis Pathway

Authors: Lucia Michielin, Lucy Havens

Text analysis allows patterns in language to be studied at scale, enabling “distant reading” of books, magazines, newspapers, and other digitized text. This pathway will guide you through the main steps and techniques needed to begin doing computational analysis of textual data.

Python VS R - What should I choose?

The last two steps of the pathway suggest the use of either Python or R to wrangle and visualise your data. Although there are more user-friendly alternatives, this is because using a coding environment will help you to streamline your workflow and make your visualisation fully reproducible.

But what would be better for your research, R or Python? The articles below discuss the pros and cons of Python and R and can help you decide which of the two languages would be the best choice for your research.
