3D Printing at UoE

The University of Edinburgh has a series of facilities and workshops with a wide variety of instrumentation that staff and students can access.

They are located in different areas of the University and they have specific regulations on how they can be used and who has access.

uCreate

1st Floor of George Square Library, Rooms 1.11 and 1.12

The uCreate Studio in George Square has an impressive array of 3D printers and equipment.

It also runs regular workshops for students and staff to learn how to use the equipment.

You can hire equipment like 3D scanners, VR lenses etc. for free from uCreate from their website.

This is the best place to start if you’re unfamiliar with arts departments.

Maltings Workshop

Minto House, Chambers Street

This workshop is chiefly for architecture students and is equipped with hand tools, woodworking machinery, laser cutters and 3D printers. Workshop induction is required for access to these workshops.

Normal ECA Workshop hours:

Monday-Friday; 10.00-12.30, 13.30-17.00

ECA Shop

74 Lauriston Place, Edinburgh

Another amazing resource is the ECA store. This is located behind the Hunter Building at the Lauriston Campus.

Have a browse of the shelves, but importantly they sell Acrylic sheets, MDF and Birch plywood which can be used for CNC milling and laser cutting.

Lauriston Campus

Within the Lauriston Campus, they have a number of excellent resources and workshops: Digital Making N1/N2, CAD embroidery, Materials and Re-Use Hub, Glass, Jewellery and Silversmithing, Textiles, Garment Printmaking, Woodworking, CNC, metal working, and casting.

Normal ECA Workshop hours:

Monday-Friday; 10.00-12.30, 13.30-17.00
