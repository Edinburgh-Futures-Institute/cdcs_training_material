Classify the text into subjective and objective

The subjective and objective classification distinguishes objective text describing facts from subjective text expressing opinions from mixed subjective and objective text. It extracts the subjective text and filters out the parts without emotion and sentiment.

What is subjective and objective classification?

Subjective and objective classification is the task of dividing text into subjective text or objective text.

Objective text is the author's objective statement: the author carries out description based on facts for incidents, objects, etc. It does not have an individual's prejudice or taste.

Subjective text means text that is described for the non-fact. It is usually our subjective evaluation of entities, time, and their attributes, and contains rich subjective opinions, emotions, opinions, attitudes, etc.

Subjective and objective classification is an essential task in NLP, or natural language processing. The automatic sorting of text into subjective and objective categories can help us analyse subjective information in the present immense text, and can support further sentiment analysis tasks.

How to classify subjective text and objective text

At the moment, the mainstream subjective and objective sorting technique is based on the supervised classification method of machine learning. The method is divided into two processes: the training process and the assorting process. In the training process, it needs subjectivity articles and objectivity articles that have been manually marked, then uses these marked samples and machine learning classification methods to train the subjective and objective sorter, such as the maximum entropy sorting technique. In the assorting process, it uses the sorter obtained to classify an unknown sample and obtain a subjective and objective classification.
