Statistical Analysis Pathway

Author: Lucia Michielin

In order to perform high-quality statistical analyses of your dataset, you'll need to go through a series of steps to organise and clean the data. This learning pathway will guide you on how to clean and analyse your data, and help you understand what relevant tools are available.

Before Starting: Data Collection

Every discipline has its own rules when it comes to data collection. In general though, it is important to also consider which type of analysis you plan to do before starting your data collection. Different analyses will require different types/qualities of data (e.g. the precision of measurements will influence the type of analysis you will be able to perform). Below you can find a series of blogs/pages with useful suggestions on how to plan and perform an efficient data collection.

  • Responsible Conduct in Data Collection
  • Data Collection Methods for Obtaining Quantitative and Qualitative Data
